Documents

Common requirements for UAE company setup.

Exact documents vary by jurisdiction and activity, but most setups begin with these basics.

  • Passport copies of shareholders
  • Visa or entry stamp copy, if applicable
  • Emirates ID for UAE residents
  • Proposed company names
  • Business activity details
  • Shareholder and manager information
