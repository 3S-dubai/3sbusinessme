The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residence programme for eligible investors, entrepreneurs, skilled professionals, scientists, outstanding students and other approved categories.

This guide explains the main UAE Golden Visa categories, benefits, documents, cost notes, application process, family sponsorship and renewal points before you start.

Official reference checkpoints

Before applying, verify your category through official UAE sources. The UAE Government portal lists eligible Golden Visa groups and ICP/GDRFA provide category-specific services and application routes.

  • UAE Government Portal - Golden Visa
  • ICP - Golden Residency services
  • GDRFA Dubai - Golden Residency services

What is the UAE Golden Visa?

The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residence permit designed to attract and retain people who contribute to the UAE’s economy, knowledge sector, investment landscape and public-interest priorities.

Important: A Golden Visa is residency, not citizenship. It does not grant a UAE passport.

Who Qualifies for the UAE Golden Visa?

Eligibility depends on the applicant’s category. The core categories below are commonly referenced by UAE official portals and residency authorities.

Investment

Investors

Applicants may qualify through public investments, business ownership, tax contribution or real estate ownership, subject to official thresholds and supporting documents.

Business

Entrepreneurs

Entrepreneurs with innovative or technical projects may qualify when supported by relevant authority, incubator or approval documents.

Talent

Scientists and exceptional talents

Scientists, specialists and exceptional talents usually require nomination, accreditation or approval from the relevant competent UAE authority.

Career

Skilled professionals

Skilled professionals may qualify where they meet employment, education, salary and occupational category requirements set by authorities.

Academic

Outstanding students

High-performing students and graduates may qualify based on academic performance and institution criteria.

Service

Humanitarian pioneers

These categories are linked to public service, humanitarian contribution and frontline recognition, usually requiring official nomination or approval.

Benefits of the UAE Golden Visa

  • Longer residency stability: Golden Residency is designed for long-term residence compared with shorter-term visas.
  • Self-sponsorship: eligible holders can hold residency without depending on a traditional employer sponsor.
  • Family sponsorship: holders can sponsor eligible family members, subject to current UAE rules.
  • Business continuity: useful for founders, investors and skilled professionals planning long-term UAE operations.

Documents Required for the UAE Golden Visa

  • Passport copy with sufficient validity
  • Current UAE visa or entry status, where applicable
  • Emirates ID, where applicable
  • Passport-size photograph
  • Medical fitness test, where required
  • Health insurance, where required
  • Category-specific evidence such as property title deed, investment proof, salary certificate, academic records or nomination letter

Step-by-Step UAE Golden Visa Application Process

  1. Identify the right category. Choose the route that matches your investment, profession, talent, academic profile or nomination.
  2. Verify current eligibility. Check official ICP, GDRFA or UAE Government guidance before preparing the application.
  3. Prepare documents. Collect standard and category-specific documents, including attestations where required.
  4. Submit through the correct channel. Applications may be processed through ICP, GDRFA Dubai, Amer centres or authorised support channels depending on the case.
  5. Track approval and issuance. Respond quickly to additional document requests or authority clarifications.

UAE Golden Visa Cost Notes

Golden Visa costs can vary by category, emirate, application channel, medical test, Emirates ID, insurance and service support.

Safe pricing rule: Treat all Golden Visa prices as indicative. Confirm the latest official quote through ICP, GDRFA, Amer or a licensed consultant before applying.

Can Golden Visa Holders Sponsor Family?

Golden Visa holders can generally sponsor eligible family members, including spouse and children, subject to current UAE authority rules. Family-member processing requires separate documents and fees.

Renewal and Validity

Golden Residency is renewable if the applicant continues to meet the eligibility conditions at renewal time. Investors and professionals should keep proof documents updated throughout the visa term.

FAQs on UAE Golden Visa

Do I need a UAE company to get a Golden Visa?

Not always. Some categories are based on real estate, talent, academic achievement or professional eligibility rather than business ownership.

Is Golden Visa approval guaranteed?

No. Approval depends on eligibility, documents and authority review.
