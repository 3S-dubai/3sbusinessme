FAQs Frequently asked questions. Clear answers on setup routes, documentation, ownership and banking requirements.
Which UAE company setup option is right for my business?
The right option depends on business activity, target market, ownership requirements, visa needs, office requirements and budget. 3S can guide you between mainland, free zone and offshore structures.
What does a business setup consultant do?
A business setup consultant helps you understand suitable jurisdictions, license requirements, estimated costs, documentation and the process involved in starting a company in Dubai or the wider UAE.
What is the difference between free zone and mainland company setup?
Free zone setup is often suitable for ownership flexibility, remote operations and structured packages. Mainland company setup is suited for businesses that need wider UAE market access and local commercial flexibility.
Can foreigners start a business in Dubai?
Yes. Foreign investors can start a business in Dubai, with 100% ownership available for many free zone and mainland activities. The suitable route depends on the chosen business activity and setup structure.
Can I open a UAE corporate bank account after company setup?
Yes. After company setup, 3S can guide you on bank account preparation, required documents and suitable banking options based on your business type and profile.
What documents may be required for UAE business setup?
Common requirements include passport copies, visa or entry stamp copies where applicable, Emirates ID for UAE residents, business activity details, proposed company names and shareholder information. Exact requirements vary by jurisdiction and activity.
What happens after the company is set up?
Post-setup requirements may include visa support, Emirates ID, corporate banking guidance, VAT or corporate tax registration, renewals and ongoing PRO services depending on the business structure.
